# Income Elasticity of Demand

## 1. What is this concept?

Income elasticity of demand measures how quantity demanded changes when consumer income changes.

It answers:

“If income increases, what happens to demand for this good?”

Formula:

Income Elasticity (YED)  
= (% change in quantity demanded)  
  / (% change in income)

---

## 2. Interpreting the sign

### Positive Income Elasticity (YED > 0)

The good is a **normal good**.

When income rises, demand rises.

Example:
Better quality food, travel, branded clothing.

People buy more as they earn more.

---

### Negative Income Elasticity (YED < 0)

The good is an **inferior good**.

When income rises, demand falls.

Example:
Very low-cost staple foods or low-end substitutes.

As people earn more, they switch away.

---

## 3. Strength of the response

The size of the elasticity matters.

### 0 < YED < 1  
Necessary goods.

Demand increases with income, but slowly.

Example:
Basic groceries.

These goods are relatively stable.

---

### YED > 1  
Luxury goods.

Demand increases more than proportionally with income.

Example:
Premium electronics, fine dining, vacations.

These industries grow fast during income booms but shrink during recessions.

---

## 4. Why this matters in decisions

Income elasticity helps answer:

- Is this product sensitive to economic cycles?
- Will growth accelerate when GDP rises?
- Is this category defensive or cyclical?
- How exposed are we to recession risk?

High income elasticity = high growth potential + high risk.

Low income elasticity = stable demand.

---

## 5. Where this shows up in analytics / business

### Market Forecasting
Linking industry growth to income or GDP growth.

### Product Strategy
Mass-market vs premium positioning.

### Portfolio Strategy
Balancing defensive and cyclical products.

### Macroeconomic Sensitivity
Estimating demand shifts during economic shocks.

---

## 6. Common misunderstandings

❌ Normal good does not mean “high quality.”
It only means demand increases with income.

❌ Inferior good does not mean “bad.”
It means demand decreases as income rises.

❌ Income elasticity is not fixed.
The same product can behave differently across income segments.

---

## 7. Simple intuition summary

When income rises:

Do people buy more of it?
Or do they upgrade away from it?

Income elasticity tells you how demand moves with prosperity.
